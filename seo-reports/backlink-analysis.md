# Backlink & Authority Strategy

## Data limitation

OpenSEO backlink APIs were **not run** (≈ credits exceed the **7 remaining**). No DR/UR/referring-domain counts are invented here.

## Qualitative authority diagnosis

- GSC positions for competitive hire terms (~70–90) are consistent with an **early-authority** domain.  
- Technical indexation is working (impression ramp) — links + brand mentions are the next constraint after on-page fixes.

## Priority link targets (ethical)

| Type | Examples of opportunities | Fit |
|------|---------------------------|-----|
| WordPress resource pages | Curated “WP agencies / developers” lists | High if editorial |
| WooCommerce partner / community | Genuine Woo community, meetups, podcasts | High |
| Guest articles | Cost guides, Elementor/Divi tutorials on relevant pubs | Medium |
| Digital PR | Case study launches, redesign before/after | Medium |
| Partner directories | Clutch/Sortlist **only if profiles are accurate** — not spam | Medium |
| Client / supplier mentions | Real client blogs, tool partners | High |
| Competitor link gap | Re-run when credits available | TBD |

## Avoid

PBNs, paid link farms, automated blog comments, manipulative guest networks, irrelevant foreign directories.

## 30–90 day authority plan

1. Claim/complete accurate profiles (Clutch etc.) with apex URL.  
2. Pitch 2 case-study stories (US + UK angle).  
3. Earn links from content that already has impressions (Woo vs Shopify, cost UK) via outreach.  
4. After credits: `get_backlinks_overview` + competitor link gap CSV refresh.
