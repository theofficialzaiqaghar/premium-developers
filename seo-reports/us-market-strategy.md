## US competitive reality (qualitative)

US “WordPress development company / hire developer” SERPs include VIP/staff-augmentation players (Multidots, rtCamp), US agencies (WordSuccor, BIG Linden, WordpressIntegration), and marketplaces. Competing as a generic “web development company USA” is **not** the near-term path.

## Recommended US angle

Position as:

> WordPress / WooCommerce / Elementor specialists for US small–mid businesses who want clear scope, async collaboration, and production-quality builds.

Tactics:

1. **SERP copy in American English** on money pages (optimize / checkout / ecommerce spelling where targeting US).  
2. **USD examples** beside GBP on pricing/cost content (honest ranges).  
3. Prioritize queries where we already have impressions: WooCommerce developer, custom WordPress development services, Elementor developer.  
4. Do **not** build state doorway pages (Texas, California, etc.).  
5. Use case studies with US clients (e.g. Lynx Med Spa) prominently on Woo/Elementor/WP services.
