## Top business insights

1. **Impressions are scaling; clicks are not.** From ~8–20 Oct (tiny) to 100–200+ impressions/day mid-Nov — indexation is working.
2. **“woocommerce developer” is the #1 commercial opportunity** (~398–416 impressions / 0 clicks / ~pos 83–87) with **cannibalization** between `/services/woocommerce-development.html` and `/blog/woocommerce-website-development-guide/`.
3. **USA already out-impresses UK** (534 vs 399) with **zero clicks** — title/meta + ranking work must be bilingual in strategy (UK messaging + US hire language).
4. **Money pages get impressions but homepage gets the clicks.** Service SEO must convert SERP visibility into clicks before conversion CRO matters.
5. **GA4 organic key events = 0.** Lead tracking for SEO is broken or unconfigured — growth cannot be managed without this.
6. **Apex primary is live** (Domains + deploy). GSC still reports many `www` rows — monitor consolidation; do not create doorway city pages.

## Positioning recommendation

Compete as a **specialist WordPress / WooCommerce / Elementor / Divi agency for UK & US SMBs**, not as a generic “web development company USA/UK” against enterprise VIP partners and Clutch-listed ecommerce agencies. Win on: clear packages, proof (case studies), response SLA, and long-tail commercial queries where KD is moderate (saved-keyword KD often 12–29 for UK WP hire terms).
