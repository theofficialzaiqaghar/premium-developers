## Competitor landscape (qualitative public sources — not our rankings)

UK WooCommerce / WordPress agency SERPs and listicles frequently feature: Atomic Smash, Kanuka Digital, The Digital Maze, Moove Agency, Wholegrain Digital, Fifteen Design, Codeable (marketplace). Project-saved competitors also include chilliapple.co.uk, impactmedia.co.uk, luminarybrands.co.uk, wpkoders.com, daveforeman.co.uk.

**Implication:** Do not fight enterprise Woo Pro Partners on “best WooCommerce agency UK” head terms first. Win **developer / development services / custom / cost / Elementor / Divi** long tails where GSC already shows impressions.

## City / local SEO

- Saved keyword `wordpress website development london` (vol ~40) exists.
- **Do not create thin London/Manchester doorway pages.**
- Instead: UK-wide service pages with clear GBP pricing signals, timezone, and remote-first delivery; optional **one** genuine local page only if you open a real office/case density.

## Irrelevant UK noise to ignore

Roofing/Bath queries (`roofers bath`, `ajh roofing`) appear due to portfolio associations — do not optimize the homepage for them.
