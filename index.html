FAQ

WordPress Development FAQs

How much does a WordPress website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, design, and features. Share your requirements through the quote form and we’ll send a clear estimate.

Can you redesign my existing WordPress site without losing SEO?

Yes. We keep your URLs, content structure, and metadata intact where possible so your rankings are protected during the redesign.

Do you work with Elementor and WooCommerce?

Yes, both are core services. We build stores, customize checkout, and optimize Elementor sites for speed.

Can you make my slow WordPress site faster?

Yes. We audit your plugins, images, CSS, and JavaScript, then fix what slows your site down and hurts Core Web Vitals.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, updates, security checks, and bug fixes.
