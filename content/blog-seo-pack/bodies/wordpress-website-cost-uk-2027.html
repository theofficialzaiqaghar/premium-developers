Planning WordPress website cost UK budgets for 2027? Figures still vary widely because a five-page brochure site and a WooCommerce store with custom checkout are different projects. This guide breaks down what you pay for, typical bands, and hidden costs owners forget.

What drives WordPress website cost in the UK

  • Scope: page count, templates, languages, and user roles
  • Design: custom layouts vs adapted theme or Elementor library
  • Functionality: forms, portals, memberships, or WooCommerce rules
  • Content: who writes and loads copy, products, and imagery
  • Integrations: CRM, booking, payments, analytics, and APIs
  • Performance and SEO: Core Web Vitals, structured data, and redirects on redesigns
  • Maintenance: updates, backups, monitoring, and support after launch

Indicative UK budget bands for 2027

These are planning ranges, not fixed prices. Every agency quotes after discovery.

  • Focused business site (5–12 pages): often roughly £4,000–£12,000 depending on design and integrations
  • Larger marketing site or multi-location brand: often £12,000–£25,000+
  • WooCommerce store: often £6,000–£25,000+ based on catalogue size and checkout rules (see our WooCommerce development page)
  • Ongoing maintenance: monthly retainers commonly £150–£600+ depending on SLAs and store complexity

Hidden costs to include

  • Premium plugins, themes, and page-builder licences
  • Hosting upgrades when traffic or store volume grows
  • Professional photography, copywriting, and legal pages
  • Payment gateway fees and app subscriptions
  • Redesign migration time to preserve SEO URLs
