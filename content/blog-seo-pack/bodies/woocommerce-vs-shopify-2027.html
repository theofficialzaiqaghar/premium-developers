WooCommerce vs Shopify in 2027 is still a control versus convenience decision for UK businesses. Both platforms evolved with better checkout tools and app ecosystems. The right choice depends on ownership, total cost, and how custom your buying journey must be.

Quick comparison for UK businesses in 2027

FactorWooCommerce (WordPress)Shopify
OwnershipYou own WordPress data and choose hostingPlatform-hosted; migration is a project
Monthly softwareHosting, plugins, licences (no core fee)Plan plus apps; transaction fees may apply
FlexibilityDeep checkout, content, and integration optionsFast standard stores; custom logic via apps and APIs
SEO and contentFull blog, guides, and landing pages on one domainGood product SEO; advanced content workflows vary
MaintenanceWordPress, WooCommerce, and plugin updatesLess server work; still test themes and apps
ScalabilityScales with hosting, architecture, and cachingScales on Shopify infrastructure

Costs UK teams should model in 2027

Shopify: subscription, payment fees, apps for reviews, subscriptions, B2B, and accounting bridges. Budget for app creep as you add features.

WooCommerce: managed WordPress hosting, premium extensions where needed, developer time for custom rules, and ongoing maintenance. Total cost of ownership can stay competitive when you need heavy content plus commerce on one site.

When WooCommerce fits in 2027

  • Content-led SEO strategy beside the shop
  • Complex UK or EU shipping, B2B pricing, or subscriptions
  • Integrations your stack already runs on WordPress
  • Long-term ownership of customer and order data

When Shopify fits in 2027

  • Rapid launch with minimal server management
  • Teams that prefer one admin for catalogue and orders
  • Catalogues that match Shopify’s common patterns

Make the decision with a roadmap

Write year-one requirements before you commit. Platform changes mid-growth are expensive. If WooCommerce is the direction, plan discovery with a developer who ships stores, not only brochure sites.
