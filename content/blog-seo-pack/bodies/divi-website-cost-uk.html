Divi website cost UK businesses ask about is rarely a single number. It depends on how much design, content, WooCommerce, migration, and ongoing support you need—not only the Divi licence itself.

What drives Divi website design cost UK

  • Page count and template complexity — marketing site vs multi-location service pages
  • Custom Divi website design vs adapting layout packs
  • Theme Builder scope — headers, footers, blog, shop templates
  • WooCommerce — product count, checkout rules, integrations
  • Content readiness — supplied copy vs copywriting support
  • Migration — from another builder or legacy shortcodes
  • Performance and SEO hygiene — redirects, schema, speed fixes

Licences and hosting (ongoing)

Elegant Themes membership covers Divi software; you still pay for domain, SSL, hosting, email, and often premium plugins (forms, SEO tools, backups). Budget these yearly, not only launch cost.

Divi developer cost UK vs DIY

DIY with layout packs is cheaper upfront but can cost more later if layouts break, speed suffers, or checkout fails. A Divi developer UK quote should spell out deliverables, revisions, and handover—not vague “website packages.”

Maintenance after launch

Updates, backups, security monitoring, and small Divi edits are often handled through maintenance & support. Factor this into total cost of ownership.
