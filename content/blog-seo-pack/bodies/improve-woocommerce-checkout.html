WooCommerce checkout optimisation targets the last step where shoppers abandon carts—often because of surprise costs, confusing forms, or slow mobile pages rather than product price alone.

Common checkout problems

  • Too many required fields or forced account creation too early
  • Shipping costs revealed late in the flow
  • Limited payment options for UK shoppers
  • Mobile layouts that hide errors or buttons
  • Slow gateway redirects or plugin conflicts

Practical improvements

Enable guest checkout where appropriate, show estimated shipping earlier, compress and defer non-critical scripts on checkout, and use clear microcopy for VAT and delivery windows.

Form design and trust

Group related fields, validate inline, and display security messaging only when backed by real SSL and reputable payment providers.

Test changes deliberately

Record baseline abandonment in analytics, change one variable at a time, and run test orders across devices. Include failed-payment and refund flows in QA.

Measurement checklist

  • Checkout funnel steps in GA4
  • Server and gateway error logs
  • Mobile vs desktop completion rate
  • Average time on checkout page
