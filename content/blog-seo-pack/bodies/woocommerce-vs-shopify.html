The WooCommerce vs Shopify decision depends on how much control you want over hosting, customization, and total cost of ownership—not on which logo is more popular.

Hosting and setup

Shopify is hosted SaaS with monthly plans and quick store setup. WooCommerce runs on WordPress, so you choose hosting and assume more setup responsibility in exchange for flexibility.

Customization

WooCommerce allows deep PHP and plugin customization, ideal when checkout, content, and SEO must share one domain. Shopify customization grows through themes, apps, and Shopify Functions within platform rules.

Costs

Shopify charges subscription plus apps and payment fees. WooCommerce software is free; costs include hosting, premium extensions, maintenance, and developer time for custom features.

Ownership and portability

WooCommerce data lives on infrastructure you control, which simplifies some migrations—though you still plan exports carefully. Shopify keeps you in its ecosystem; exiting requires structured migration projects.

Maintenance

WooCommerce stores need WordPress core, plugin, and security updates. Shopify handles platform updates but still requires app hygiene and theme testing.

Which fits whom?

  • Consider Shopify when you want managed hosting and faster standard ecommerce launch with less dev overhead.
  • Consider WooCommerce when content marketing, custom checkout, or unique integrations are central to growth.

Neither platform is universally “better”—align the choice with your team’s technical capacity and five-year roadmap.
