A thoughtful business website redesign can improve trust, conversions, and search performance—but timing matters. Redesign too late and you lose leads; redesign without a plan and you can lose valuable SEO equity.

10 signs your website needs a professional redesign

  1. The layout looks dated compared to competitors in your industry.
  2. Mobile usability is poor—buttons overlap, text is tiny, forms fail.
  3. Navigation is confusing; users cannot find services or pricing paths.
  4. Page speed scores are consistently weak on real devices.
  5. Branding no longer matches your current services or audience.
  6. Conversion paths are unclear—few CTAs or too many competing links.
  7. Content is duplicated, outdated, or buried in PDFs.
  8. Accessibility issues block users with assistive technology.
  9. Adding new pages is painful because of technical debt.
  10. Analytics show high bounce rates on key landing pages.

Redesign without losing SEO

Map existing URLs, implement 301 redirects for changed slugs, preserve high-performing content, and relaunch with updated internal links. Avoid deleting service pages that rank without replacements.

Practical redesign checklist

  • Audit top landing pages and backlinks in Search Console
  • Define primary conversion goals (calls, forms, bookings)
  • Wireframe mobile-first layouts
  • Plan content migration and a redirect spreadsheet
  • Test forms, analytics, and indexing after launch
