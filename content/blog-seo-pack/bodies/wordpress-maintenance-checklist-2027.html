Use this WordPress website maintenance checklist for 2027 to keep your business site secure, fast, and editable. Small teams often launch successfully, then let updates slip until forms, checkout, or search rankings suffer.

Weekly checks

  • Test contact and lead forms; confirm emails arrive
  • For stores, place a test order on staging or a low-value live order
  • Review uptime alerts and downtime logs from your host
  • Scan comments, login attempts, and security plugin notices

Monthly maintenance

  • Updates: WordPress core, plugins, and themes on staging first, then production
  • Backups: verify automated backups; run a restore test quarterly
  • Security: review admin users, weak passwords, and unused plugins
  • Speed: check Core Web Vitals on key URLs (see speed guide)
  • Forms and integrations: CRM, booking, and payment webhooks still connected
  • Database health: remove spam, limit autoloaded options, and monitor size

Quarterly tasks

  • Audit plugin list; deactivate and remove unused tools
  • Review SSL certificate renewal and mixed-content warnings
  • Check legal pages, cookie consent, and privacy copy still match tracking
  • Refresh hero offers, seasonal shipping notes, and opening hours

When to hire maintenance support

If updates feel risky, you rely on WooCommerce revenue, or nobody owns backups, a retainer is cheaper than emergency recovery. Document what is in scope: updates only, content tweaks, performance, or security response.
