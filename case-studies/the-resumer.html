Case study

The Resumer

Website design and development for a WordPress and Elementor site that presents professional CV writing, LinkedIn profile management, and content writing services—and gives visitors a clear way to inquire by contact form.

  • Client: The Resumer
  • Platform: WordPress
  • Page builder: Elementor
  • Live site: theresumer.co.uk
View live website → Start your project

1. Project overview

Premium Developers delivered website design and development for The Resumer (theresumer.co.uk). The site runs on WordPress with layouts built in Elementor. Our scope was the website itself: presenting the business services and supporting customer inquiries through a contact form—not automated CV generation or custom application development beyond the verified build scope.

2. About the business

The Resumer provides professional career and content services, including:

  • Professional CV writing
  • LinkedIn profile management
  • Content writing

The website’s role is to explain these offerings and encourage visitors to get in touch when they need support.

3. Project goals

Based on the agreed development scope, the website was intended to:

  • Present The Resumer’s services in a clear, professional way
  • Reflect a credible brand presence suitable for a services business
  • Provide a straightforward contact path for customer inquiries
  • Remain manageable on WordPress with Elementor for ongoing content updates

Owner verification: Detailed pre-launch KPIs, marketing targets, or internal success metrics were not supplied in the materials available for this case study. Add them here if you want them published.

4. The challenge

The Resumer offers several related services that need to be easy to understand at a glance. Visitors should quickly see what the business does, trust the presentation, and know how to inquire—without confusion between service lines or a unclear contact path.

Owner verification: Specific pre-project pain points (for example, legacy branding or prior platform constraints) were not documented in the verified handover notes used for this page.

5. Our approach

We approached the project as a structured WordPress and Elementor build:

  • Align page structure and content areas with the three core service lines
  • Design layouts that read clearly on desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Implement a contact form as the primary inquiry channel
  • Hand over a site the client can maintain through WordPress and Elementor

6. Website design & development

Design and development work focused on visual hierarchy, readable service sections, and consistent branding across key pages. Elementor was used to build responsive sections while keeping editing practical for non-developers.

We did not build an automated CV builder, customer portal, or bespoke SaaS product as part of the verified scope for this case study.

7. Services presented on the website

The site content is organized around the business services confirmed for this project:

  1. Professional CV writing — presenting how The Resumer supports clients with CV documents.
  2. LinkedIn profile management — explaining LinkedIn-focused profile support.
  3. Content writing — covering written content services aligned with the brand.

8. Contact form & email inquiry system

Customer inquiries are handled through a contact form on the website. When a visitor submits the form, the submission is delivered to the client’s email so The Resumer can review and respond. This is a standard inquiry workflow—not an automated quoting engine, CRM integration, or instant booking system unless separately configured by the client after launch.

Owner verification: Exact form plugin name, email routing rules, and notification addresses can be added by the site owner if they should appear on this case study.

9. Responsive design

Layouts were implemented to adapt across common screen sizes so service information and contact paths remain usable on phones, tablets, and desktops. Elementor’s responsive controls were used to tune spacing and stacking where needed.

10. Final outcome

The published website at theresumer.co.uk presents The Resumer’s services and contact pathway on WordPress and Elementor. Premium Developers’ verified contribution is website design and development for that presence.

We do not publish traffic, conversion, revenue, SEO ranking, or performance score claims for this project without measured evidence and client approval.
