Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit Premium Developers or contact us about our services.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

About this policy

“Premium Developers,” “we,” and “us” refer to the people operating this website and providing the services described here. This policy applies to information handled through this website and through direct contact initiated from it.

Information you choose to share

If you use the contact form, we receive the details you submit, such as your name, email address, website URL, preferred project timeline, inquiry type, and message. The form sends that information through our email delivery provider, Resend, to hello@premiumdevelopers.co and muhammadsaadullah093@gmail.com so we can respond. If you email us directly or contact us through a linked service such as Upwork or Contra, we may receive the information you choose to provide there as well.

If you submit the optional exit feedback offer, we store your email address, selected reason, optional comments, page path, submission time, and promotional email preference in Upstash Redis, and send the discount code you requested through Resend. The code email is sent whether or not you opt in; this form does not subscribe you to ongoing promotional messages. Feedback records are automatically removed after the configured retention period (365 days by default).

If you submit a special-offer inquiry, we receive your name, business email, website URL, preferred project timeline, selected services, project details, and any optional business name, phone number, or budget you provide. We store the inquiry in Upstash Redis when available and send its details through Resend to hello@premiumdevelopers.co and muhammadsaadullah093@gmail.com so we can respond. If Redis is unavailable, the email notifications serve as the inquiry record in those recipient inboxes. Redis-stored inquiries are automatically removed after the configured retention period (365 days by default). The inquiry does not subscribe you to ongoing marketing messages.

We use that information to respond to you, discuss or provide requested services, and keep records needed to manage our communications and work. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Information collected by the website

The site does not ask you to create an account. When you browse pages, our hosting provider (Vercel) may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs to deliver the site, protect it, and troubleshoot problems. We do not control the hosting provider’s infrastructure or retention settings.

When you submit the contact form, a service-page project inquiry, the homepage inquiry form, exit feedback, or a special-offer inquiry, the information described elsewhere in this policy is processed so we can respond to your request.

Analytics, advertising, and Google services

We use Google Tag Manager (container ID GTM-WF285DKZ) on this website. Through Tag Manager we may load tags for Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Ads conversion measurement, and related Google marketing technologies. These tools help us understand how visitors use the site, measure form submissions and other actions, and evaluate advertising performance.

Google and other providers may set or read cookies or similar technologies on your device when those tags are allowed to run. Data processed may include pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser information, referral source, and events such as successful form submissions or clicks on contact actions (for example WhatsApp). We configure the site to use Google Consent Mode so analytics and advertising storage stay denied until you accept non-essential cookies through our cookie banner, or until you save custom choices.

For more information about how Google uses data, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

Legal bases (UK / EEA visitors): We rely on your consent for non-essential analytics and advertising cookies and similar technologies. Responding to inquiries you send us is based on our legitimate interest in operating our business and taking steps at your request before a contract, or on consent where you tick an optional marketing box. You may withdraw cookie consent at any time using Cookie settings in the site footer.

Third-party services and links

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and service-page photographs from Unsplash. Your browser may connect directly to those providers to retrieve those assets. Contact-form, special-offer inquiry, and requested discount-code emails are transmitted through Resend. Exit-offer feedback and special-offer inquiries are stored in Upstash Redis, and Vercel hosts the website and form endpoints; these providers may process request metadata for delivery and abuse prevention. Analytics and advertising tags are served through Google Tag Manager as described above. The site links to external portfolio websites and services, including Upwork and Contra. When you follow an external link, that provider’s privacy terms apply; review them before sharing information.

Cookies and storage

Essential / functional storage: The exit-feedback offer uses browser local storage to remember when it was last shown (normally once every 30 days). The promotional offer popup uses session storage to appear no more than once per browsing session; if session storage is unavailable, it may use a short-lived session cookie. Our cookie consent banner stores your choice in local storage under the key pd_cookie_consent_v1.

Analytics and advertising cookies: When you accept analytics or marketing cookies, Google tags loaded via Tag Manager may set cookies such as those used by GA4 or Google Ads for measurement and, if enabled in your Google accounts, remarketing. These are blocked by default until you consent.

You can change or withdraw consent using Cookie settings in the footer, or manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking storage may affect pop-up frequency, form convenience, or measurement.

Vercel, Resend, Upstash, and Google may process technical request information as part of delivering and protecting their services.

Retention and your choices

We keep direct correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project, maintain appropriate business records, or resolve a dispute. Exit-offer feedback and special-offer inquiries are removed automatically after their configured retention periods (365 days by default). You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent, subject to any obligations that require us to retain it.

Children and policy changes

This website and its services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
