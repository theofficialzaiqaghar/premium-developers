Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit Premium Developers or contact us about our services.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

About this policy

“Premium Developers,” “we,” and “us” refer to the people operating this website and providing the services described here. This policy applies to information handled through this website and through direct contact initiated from it.

Information you choose to share

If you use the contact form, we receive the details you submit, such as your name, email address, inquiry type, and message. The form sends that information through our email delivery provider, Resend, to our inquiry inbox so we can respond. If you email us directly or contact us through a linked service such as Upwork or Contra, we may receive the information you choose to provide there as well.

We use that information to respond to you, discuss or provide requested services, and keep records needed to manage our communications and work. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Information collected by the website

We have not intentionally added advertising trackers or analytics scripts to this website. The website itself does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information through a form.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser, and request logs to deliver the site, protect it, and troubleshoot problems. We do not control the hosting provider’s infrastructure or retention settings.

Third-party services and links

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and service-page photographs from Unsplash. Your browser may connect directly to those providers to retrieve those assets. Contact-form submissions are transmitted through Resend for email delivery. We also use Vercel to host the website and its form endpoint; its infrastructure may process request metadata for delivery and abuse prevention. The site links to external portfolio websites and services, including Upwork and Contra. When you follow an external link, that provider’s privacy terms apply; review them before sharing information.

Cookies and storage

The site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Vercel and Resend may process technical request information as part of delivering and protecting their services. You can manage browser cookies through your browser settings, though blocking some third-party resources may affect how pages display.

Retention and your choices

We keep direct correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project, maintain appropriate business records, or resolve a dispute. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent, subject to any obligations that require us to retain it.

Children and policy changes

This website and its services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@premiumdevelopers.co.
