Planning your budget

WooCommerce store pricing guidance

Every catalogue and checkout is different; we quote after a short discovery. These bands help UK and US teams plan:

Starter store

Focused catalogue, standard checkout, essential payment and shipping—often from roughly £6,000–£12,000 depending on products and design.

Growth store

Custom layouts, integrations, subscriptions or B2B rules—typically £12,000–£25,000+ with phased delivery.

Improvements

Checkout, catalogue, or performance upgrades on an existing store—scoped as fixed phases or days, quoted after review.

Indicative figures exclude VAT, licences, and content entry. Read our WooCommerce vs Shopify UK guide and essential store features article.

Common questions

WooCommerce developer FAQ

What does a WooCommerce developer do?

A WooCommerce developer configures and customizes your WordPress store: product catalogues, cart and checkout, payments, shipping, and integrations that match how you sell.

Do you build new stores or improve existing ones?

Both. We can launch a new store from agreed requirements or refine an existing WooCommerce site—checkout flow, product presentation, performance, and admin workflows.

Can you connect payments, shipping, and other tools?

Yes, within the scope we agree upfront. We focus on reliable payment and shipping setup plus integrations that support day-to-day operations.

How do I get started?

Use our contact form to share your products, audience, and timeline. We aim to reply within two business days.

How much does WooCommerce development cost?

Costs depend on product count, checkout rules, and integrations. After discovery we provide a fixed quote or phased plan—see the pricing guidance on this page and our WooCommerce development guide.

Can you set up UK payments and shipping?

Yes, within agreed scope. We configure gateways such as Stripe and PayPal, shipping zones, and tax behaviour appropriate to how you sell in the UK, EU, or US.

How long does a WooCommerce launch take?

Simple stores may take a few weeks after content is ready; larger catalogues or custom checkout often run in phases. We agree milestones before build starts.
