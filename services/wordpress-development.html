Common questions

WordPress developer FAQ

What does a WordPress developer do for a business website?

A WordPress developer plans and builds your site structure, templates, and key functionality so content is clear, layouts work on mobile and desktop, and day-to-day updates stay practical for your team.

Do you build new WordPress sites or improve existing ones?

Both—we can launch a new WordPress site from agreed requirements or refine an existing installation: navigation, page layouts, integrations, and performance based on what you need next.

Can you connect forms, tools, or third-party services?

Yes, within the scope we agree upfront. We focus on reliable forms, practical integrations, and features that match how your business operates without unnecessary complexity.

How do I start a WordPress project with Premium Developers?

Share your goals, content, and any reference sites through our contact form. We reply with practical next steps, usually within two business days.

How much does a WordPress website cost in the UK?

Do you work with UK and US businesses remotely?

Yes. We are a remote WordPress agency serving clients in the United Kingdom, United States, and internationally via email, video, and staging reviews.
